tpt-fluids Design Specification (spec.txt)
1. Overview
Name: tpt-fluids
Description: A pure-Rust, AI-native applied fluid mechanics library. It consolidates three specialized fluid domains — 1D hydraulic networks, marine hydrodynamics, and tribology — into a single feature-gated workspace, without C/C++ FFI or external solver dependencies.
License: MIT (all dependencies must be MIT or compatible; no Apache-2.0-only crates).
Core Philosophy: Unit-safe, no_std + alloc compatible at the core, differentiable for gradient-based design optimization, and mathematically rigorous via native Rust verification tooling and from-scratch implementations.
2. Design Principles
Zero External Solver Dependencies: No EPANET FFI, no WAMIT, no external tribology solvers. All network solvers, resistance integrals, and Reynolds equation discretizations are implemented from scratch in pure Rust, leveraging tpt-math for linear algebra and ODE solving.
Strict Licensing: No Apache-2.0-only dependencies. The workspace strictly uses MIT, MIT/Apache-2.0 dual, or custom in-house crates.

tpt-fluids-hydraulic
Purpose: 1D pipe network analysis for civil, mechanical, and process engineering.
Key Features:
Steady-state networks: Darcy-Weisbach and Hazen-Williams friction models, Colebrook-White iterative solver, minor loss coefficients.
Network solvers: Hardy Cross method (loop-based) and Global Gradient Algorithm (node-based Newton-Raphson).
Transient analysis: Water hammer via Method of Characteristics (MOC), Joukowsky equation, column separation modeling.
Component models: Pump/turbine characteristic curves (four-quadrant), valve Cv models, cavitation inception, surge tank dynamics.
Topology: Directed graph representation with node/edge/loop extraction (leveraging tpt-graph if available).
Differentiable head-loss functions for network optimization (e.g., pipe sizing).
tpt-fluids-marine
Purpose: Naval architecture and marine hydrodynamics for ships and underwater vehicles.
Key Features:
Resistance prediction:
Frictional: ITTC-1957 model-ship correlation line, Granville method.
Residuary: Michell's thin-ship integral, Holtrop-Mennen empirical method.
Air drag, appendage drag, and correlation allowance.
Froude scaling: Model-to-ship extrapolation with form factor and roughness allowance.
Seakeeping: Linear wave theory, Response Amplitude Operators (RAOs) for 6-DOF ship motions, significant wave height statistics.
Maneuvering: MMG (Mathematical Modeling Group) model for 3-DOF horizontal plane motions (surge, sway, yaw), hydrodynamic derivatives.
Propulsion: Wake fraction, thrust deduction, propeller-hull interaction, open-water diagrams.
Froude-Krylov excitation: For underwater vehicles and floating structures.
tpt-fluids-tribo
Purpose: Lubrication theory, contact mechanics, and wear modeling.
Key Features:
Hydrodynamic lubrication: Reynolds equation solver (1D/2D finite difference) for journal bearings, slider bearings, and thrust bearings.
Elastohydrodynamic Lubrication (EHL): Coupled Reynolds + elastic deformation for gears and rolling-element bearings, Dowson-Hampton film thickness formulas.
Regime modeling: Stribeck curve navigation (boundary, mixed, hydrodynamic regimes).
Wear modeling: Archard's equation, adhesive/abrasive wear coefficients, running-in simulation.
Friction models: Coulomb, Stribeck, LuGre dynamic friction for multibody joint integration.
Differentiable load-capacity integrals for bearing geometry optimization.

5. Integration & Synergies
tpt-physics (CFD): tpt-fluids provides fast, specialized 1D and semi-empirical solvers. tpt-physics is reserved for full 3D Navier-Stokes where specialization fails (e.g., complex free-surface flows, turbulent separation).
tpt-aero: Sibling specialization. tpt-aero owns air (VLM, BEMT); tpt-fluids owns water, pipes, and thin films. Both share the tpt-fluids-core unit-safe types for non-dimensional numbers.
tpt-fem: tpt-fluids-tribo's EHL module couples Reynolds equation with tpt-fem-elasticity for elastic deformation of bearing surfaces.
tpt-multibody-dynamics: tpt-fluids-marine provides hydrodynamic forces (resistance, Froude-Krylov, maneuvering) as external loads on ship 6-DOF models. tpt-fluids-tribo provides friction models for joint damping.
tpt-materials: Supplies temperature-dependent viscosity, density, and surface roughness data.
tpt-systems-optimisation: Powers gradient-based optimization of pipe network sizing, hull form optimization (via differentiable Michell integral), and bearing geometry design.
tpt-construction / tpt-engineering: Direct consumers of tpt-fluids-hydraulic for HVAC, plumbing, and municipal water network design.
AI-Native Agents: Differentiable solvers allow tpt-eve / tpt-anima agents to perform inverse design (e.g., "find the hull form that minimizes resistance at 15 knots").

Phase 2: Tribology & Seakeeping (Weeks 7-9)
Build tpt-fluids-tribo (Reynolds equation solver, Stribeck curve).
Implement seakeeping RAOs and MMG maneuvering model in tpt-fluids-marine.
Write Kani proofs for MOC CFL stability and Reynolds load equilibrium.
Phase 3: Advanced Coupling (Weeks 10-12)
EHL coupling with tpt-fem-elasticity.
Pump/turbine four-quadrant curves and cavitation modeling.
End-to-end test: Optimize pipe network diameters using tpt-systems-optimisation.
